Move preset navigator

M — the Menu button. It opens the preset screen at the left of the slots.
Shift+M — the Menu long press. It opens the save input.

On the open screen: the mouse wheel or arrows walk the list, and every row you rest on plays right away — watch the sliders. Enter (the jog click) or a row click keeps it. Backspace (the Back button) or an M tap puts your old settings back and dismisses. Hold C (the Mute button) to hear your old settings while you browse; release to return to the preview.

Some presets are saved for you already. Turn a slot, save your own, and load one back.
